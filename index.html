AFTERT
Fly fighter jets. Or just watch them roar past — and hear the Doppler effect.
Best with headphones · Chrome, Edge or Firefox on desktop

Controls

Flying

W S / ↑ ↓Pitch (S / ↓ = pull up)

Watching flybys

SpaceLaunch the plane (it only flies when you press this)
RReset the plane
Mouse dragLook around (turns off auto-track)
Mouse wheelZoom (binoculars)
TAuto-track camera on / off
ZAuto zoom on / off
DWave diagram & graphs on / off
BdB (sound level) meter on / off
MDoppler effect on / off (compare!)
HHide / show panels
CCamera: ground ↔ riding the jet
EscPause / menu

Gamepad

Left stick: roll / pitch · Right stick X: rudder · RT: afterburner · LT: brake · A: cannon · B: missile

Settings

Advanced graphics

Graphics changes take effect when you press Apply. Ultra needs a strong graphics card.

Replays

Press G while flying to record a flight. Keep up to three replays: save the latest flight into a slot, overwrite a slot, or delete it.
